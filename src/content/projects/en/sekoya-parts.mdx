- **Scale.** 100,000 products, 60 brands and 1,000,000 appliance models, made by a seeded generator that always gives the same catalog.
- **A product that fits a million appliances.** Its page lists the most popular models, and the rest on numbered pages read straight from an index: the last page is as fast as the first.
- **Partial refresh.** Each part of a page (price, description, reference list) is cached on its own. Editing a price re-renders the price only.
- **Ten languages** on one domain, with translated addresses and hreflang tags.
- **Performance as a merge gate.** Lighthouse measures each pull request's preview, which cannot merge below the set threshold.
- **A demonstration shop:** the cart, customer accounts and a mock checkout work, but nothing is paid or delivered. No analytics, no third-party script.
